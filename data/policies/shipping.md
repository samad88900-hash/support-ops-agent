# Shipping Policy

- Standard delivery takes **2–4 working days** inside Pakistan.
- Orders above **PKR 5,000** ship free; below that delivery is **PKR 199**.
- We ship through **TCS** and **Leopards**. Tracking is shared once the order is packed.
- Remote areas may take one extra working day.
- Delivery attempts: courier tries twice; after that the parcel returns to our warehouse.
